**The Eight Dimensions of Well-being: A Holistic Framework**
The eight dimensions of well-being, as outlined by the World Health Organization
(WHO), provide a comprehensive framework for understanding the complex interplay
between physical, emotional, social, and mental health. These dimensions include:
1. **Physical**: Good physical health and well-being
2. **Mental**: Good mental health and well-being
3. **Social**: Good relationships and social connections
4. **Emotional**: Good emotional health and well-being
5. **Spiritual**: A sense of purpose and meaning
6. **Environmental**: A healthy and sustainable environment
7. **Financial**: Financial security and stability
8. **Occupational**: A ful lling and engaging career
