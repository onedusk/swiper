5. App listing
The app listing is your calling card - it helps merchants find your app and understand how it can help
them run their businesses. Your listing explains the features, user interface, and functionality of your app.
Your listing should clearly communicate functionality and pricing so that merchants can quickly
understand the benefits of your app.

Writing a Shopify App Store listing
The app listing is your first point of contact with a merchant, and it's where they'll go to determine
whether your app is right for them. All approved public apps have a listing on the Shopify App Store,
regardless of whether you choose to make it full or limited visibility.
The listing is often your biggest marketing tool—an effective app listing encourages Shopify merchants to
try your app for themselves. Make sure that your app listing is clear, and that it answers the questions that
a potential user might have.
To create a Shopify App Store listing, you first need to select Shopify App Store as the distribution
method for your app.
The app listing submission form lets you do the following:
highlight app features so merchants can easily see what your app can do for them
provide clear pricing information
specify which merchants can install your app
