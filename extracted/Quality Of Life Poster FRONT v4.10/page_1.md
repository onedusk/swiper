the others, and ultimately one's health, well-being, and quality of life.
Important note: They do not have to be equally balanced.

Completing a wellness wheel chart is
a great way to assess your overall
well-being. To get started, use the
blank wellness wheel chart above. The
chart is divided into 8 sections, each
representing a different area of wellness. Next, you’ll need to rate yourself
on a scale of 1-10 in each area. For
example, if you feel like you are in
good physical health, you might rate
yourself an 8. If you feel like you are
struggling emotionally, you might rate
yourself a 3. Once you have rated
yourself in each area, you can connect
the dots to create a visual representation of your overall well-being.

Finally, you can use your wellness wheel
chart to identify areas where you may
need to focus more attention. For
example, if you rated yourself low in the
social or environmental categories, you
may want to work on connecting with
friends and family or spending more
time outdoors. Once you have identified
areas to focus on, you can create goals
and action plans to help you reach your
desired level of well-being.
