Founder equity terms

Learn about selecting your equity vesting terms.
Atlas defaults to industry-standard founder equity vesting terms. However, you can customize your vesting
period and vesting start dates, and choose whether to include a vesting cliff.

Standard equity terms

As a founder, you need to understand the following equity terms:
Vesting: The process by which you gradually gain ownership of assets such as company equity over
time, rather than receiving them all at once.
Vesting period: The total length of time over which you earn your founder equity. If you leave
before the end of the vesting period, you only keep the stock that has already vested.
Vesting cliffs: The date before which no equity vests. If you leave before the cliff date, you won’t
receive any stock.
Vesting start date: The date when your vesting period begins.
Most founders set up their companies with a four-year vesting period and a one-year cliff, with a vesting
start date set as the date of incorporation. All Atlas founder equity vests monthly. For example, with a fouryear vesting period, founders receive 1/48 of their total grant each month.
Here’s what these standard terms mean for your startup:
Four-year vesting period: Your founder equity vests in monthly increments over four years.
One-year cliff: No equity vests until you have been with the company for a full year. With a fouryear vesting period, 25% of shares vest immediately on the cliff date.
Vesting start date as day of incorporation: Vesting begins the day your company is incorporated.

Select your equity terms

As a C Corp founder, you can select equity vesting terms that differ from the industry standard.
You can select your equity terms for your founding team in the Atlas application:
1. In your Atlas application in the Dashboard, go to the Equity terms page.
2. Select the equity vesting period for your entire founding team.
3. For each founder, choose between a one-year cliff or no cliff. However, a one-year cliff is typically
the industry standard and preferred by investors.
4. Select a vesting start date up to 6 months before the current date for each founder.
5. Review selections on the application summary page before you submit your application.
Note
The founder who submits your Atlas application selects the equity terms for all team members. We
recommend that your team discusses your desired equity terms before submitting the application.
