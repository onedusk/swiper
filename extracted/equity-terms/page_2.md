Vesting cliff

A vesting cliff is an initial period during which no shares vest.
You can use Atlas to customize vesting cliffs for each founder:
One-year cliff (default): You must stay for one year before any shares vest. For example, in a fouryear vesting schedule, after this initial one-year period, 25% of shares vest immediately, with the
remainder vesting monthly.
No cliff: Shares begin vesting immediately according to the monthly vesting schedule, without a
waiting period.
Founders might remove the cliff because the team wants to be rewarded for their work regardless of how
long they remain with the startup. However, a one-year cliff is the industry standard and is generally
preferred by investors.

Vesting start date

The vesting start date determines when your vesting period begins.
You can use Atlas to customize the vesting start date for each founder:
The date of incorporation (default)
Up to 6 months before the date of incorporation
You might set a vesting start date prior to incorporation to acknowledge work completed for the startup
before it was officially incorporated.

Limitations

Atlas doesn’t support equity customization beyond the options listed above. For questions and feedback on
customizations, contact us at atlas-feedback@stripe.com.
