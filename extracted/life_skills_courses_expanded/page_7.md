Program Philosophy and Prerequisites
Universal Access Principle
Core Prerequisite: You are a human being and are breathing.
This program is built on the fundamental belief that every person, regardless of background,
education level, or life circumstances, has the capacity to learn, grow, and improve their life skills. We
reject artificial barriers to education and embrace the following principles:
Inclusive Design Features:
No educational prerequisites required
No age restrictions (courses adapted for ages 14+)
No prior experience necessary
No technology expertise assumed
Multiple language options available
Accessibility features for all abilities
Financial assistance for those in need
What You Actually Need:
A desire to improve your life
Willingness to try new things
Access to internet (library access counts)
2-3 hours per week to invest in yourself
An open mind
We Welcome:
First-time online learners
People returning to education after decades
Non-native English speakers
Individuals with learning differences
Those recovering from life setbacks
Career changers at any age
Young people planning their futures
Retirees seeking new purpose
