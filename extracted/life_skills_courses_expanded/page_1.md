Executive Summary
The global shift towards holistic education has created an unprecedented opportunity for life skills
education. According to the World Economic Forum's "The Future of Jobs Report 2020," analytical
thinking, creativity, and flexibility are among the top skills employers will seek by 2025. The same
report indicates that 50% of all employees will need reskilling by 2025 as technology adoption
increases. Additionally, the OECD's Survey of Adult Skills (PIAAC) found that only 31% of adults
possess problem-solving skills in technology-rich environments at proficiency levels 2 or 3. This
comprehensive framework outlines a research-based approach to developing online life skills courses
that address the eight dimensions of well-being as defined by SAMHSA (Substance Abuse and
Mental Health Services Administration).
