Surveying Software and Web Sites
You can access two major repositories of personal finance information
through your computer. Although the lines are blurring between these two
categories, they’re roughly defined as software and the Internet:
✓ Software refers to computer programs that are either packaged in a
box or DVD case or are available to be downloaded online. Most of the
mass-marketed financial software packages sell for under $100. If you’ve
ever used a word-processing program such as Word or WordPerfect or a
spreadsheet program such as Excel, then you’ve used software.
