Chapter 22: Ten Tactics to Thwart Identity Theft and Fraud

Protect Your Mail
Some identity thieves have collected personal information by simply helping
themselves to mail in home mailboxes. Especially if your mail is delivered to
a curbside box, stealing mail is pretty easy.
Consider using a locked mailbox or a post office box to protect your incoming
mail from theft. Consider having your investment and other important statements sent to you via e-mail, or simply access them online and eliminate mail
delivery of the paper copies.
Be careful with your outgoing mail as well, such as bills with checks attached.
Minimize your outgoing mail and save yourself hassles by signing up for automatic bill payment for as many bills as you are able. Drop the rest of your
outgoing mail in a secure U.S. postal box, such as those you find at the post
office.
