Crunching numbers for your retirement
Now that you’ve toured the components of your future retirement income,
take a shot at tallying where you stand in terms of retirement preparations.
Don’t be afraid to do this exercise — it’s not difficult, and you may find that
you’re not in such bad shape. I even explain how to catch up if you find that
you’re behind in saving for retirement.
