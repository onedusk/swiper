Arabesque
—Created at La Griglia, Houston, Texas.

5 strawberries, hulled
1 ounce citrus vodka
1 ounce Campari
1 ounce champagne or other sparkling wine

In a blender, combine the strawberries, vodka, and Campari with
a few ice cubes. Pour into a Champagne Flute and top with the
champagne.

Arawak Cocktail
—Created at Trotters, Port of Spain, Trinidad.

2 ounces blended whisky
1/2 ounce dry vermouth
1/4 ounce pineapple juice

Dash of tamarind juice
2 dashes of Angostura bitters
Pineapple cube, for garnish

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Add the
