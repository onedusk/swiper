Rum Fizz
This drink can be made with lemon juice instead of lime
juice if you so desire.
2 ounces dark rum
1 ounce fresh lime juice
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

5 to 6 ounces club soda
Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the rum, lime juice, and simple syrup into a shaker twothirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled wine goblet. Add the club soda; stir briefly. Add the garnish of choice.

Rum Julep
1 ounce simple syrup (page 33)
3 ounces dark rum
3 large mint sprigs, for garnish

Pour the simple syrup into a crushed ice–filled Julep Cup; stir
well. Add the rum; stir until a film of ice forms on the exterior of
the Julep Cup. Add the mint garnish.
