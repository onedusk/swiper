Big Pine Key Cocktail
—Adapted from a recipe by Robert (Bobbo) Semmes, a cocktail
aficionado from South Carolina.

1 1/2 ounces gin
1 ounce Cointreau
3/4 ounce fresh lime juice

Orange twist, for garnish

Pour the gin, Cointreau, and lime juice into a shaker two-thirds
full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
Add the orange twist garnish.

Bijou Cocktail
This one’s a jewel.
1 1/2 ounces gin
1/2 ounce sweet vermouth
1/4 ounce green Chartreuse

2 dashes of orange bitters

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
