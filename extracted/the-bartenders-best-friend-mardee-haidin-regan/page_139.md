Cinco de Mayo
Created to celebrate the May 5, 1862, victory of the
Mexican Army over the French at the Battle of Puebla.
2 1/2 ounces añejo or white tequila
1 ounce fresh lime juice
1/2 ounce grenadine

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Cinderella

C

3 ounces orange juice
3 ounces peach nectar
Dash of grenadine
Splash of club soda

Pour the orange juice, peach nectar, and grenadine into an icefilled Collins glass. Stir briefly; top with the club soda.

Cinnamon Cream Martini
2 ounces Stolichnaya Vanil vodka
1/2 ounce Goldschlager

Pour both ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Claridge Cocktail
Could this be named after the famous London hotel?
Maybe. But we know that it was served at London’s
Savoy Hotel, circa 1930.
1 1/2 ounces gin
1 1/2 ounces dry vermouth
3/4 ounce Cointreau
3/4 ounce apricot brandy

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
