Cherries Jubilee Martini
—From The Purple Martini, Denver, Colorado.

3 ounces Ketel One vodka
Splash of amaretto
Maraschino cherry, for garnish

Pour the vodka and amaretto into a mixing glass two-thirds full
of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Add the
cherry.

Cherry Blossom Cocktail
A spring drink to be sure.
1 1/2 ounces brandy
1 ounce cherry brandy
1/2 ounce triple sec

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Cherry Brandy Rickey
A grown-up version of the Shirley Temple?
2 1/2 ounces cherry brandy
1 ounce fresh lime juice
6 to 8 ounces club soda
Lime wedge, for garnish

Pour the cherry brandy and lime juice into an ice-filled Highball
or Collins glass. Pour in the club soda; stir briefly. Add the lime
wedge.
