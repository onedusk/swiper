Bosom Caresser
The racy name of this drink dates to the late 1800s.
2 ounces brandy
1 ounce white curaçao
Dash of grenadine
1 egg yolk

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Shake very well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Boston Cocktail
1 1/2 ounces gin
1 1/2 ounces apricot brandy
1/4 ounce fresh lemon juice

Splash of grenadine

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Bourbon & Branch
The recipe that answers the question: “What is ‘branch
water’ anyway?”
2 1/2 ounces bourbon
4 to 5 ounces still spring water

Pour the bourbon and water into an ice-filled Highball glass. Stir
briefly.

Bourbon Cobbler
If you make this drink when blueberries are plentiful, use
them as a garnish—they marry very well to the bourbon.
2 1/2 ounces bourbon
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the bourbon and simple syrup into a wine goblet filled with
crushed ice. Stir briefly. Add the garnish of choice.
