Beachcomber
A drink favored by “Trader Vic” Bergeron.
Superfine sugar and a lime wedge, for rimming the glass
2 ounces light rum
1/4 ounce maraschino liqueur

B

1/4 ounce cherry brandy
1/2 ounce fresh lime juice

Use the sugar and lime wedge to coat the exterior rim of a Cocktail
glass. Pour all of the remaining ingredients into a shaker two-thirds
full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into the sugar-rimmed glass.

Beadlestone Cocktail
2 ounces scotch
1 ounce dry vermouth
Dash of Angostura bitters

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Beam Me Up Scotty
1/2 ounce Kahlúa
1/2 ounce crème de banane
1/2 ounce Baileys Irish cream liqueur

Layer in order in a Pony glass.

Beauty Spot Cocktail
1 ounce gin
1/2 ounce dry vermouth
1/2 ounce sweet vermouth
1/4 ounce orange juice

1 egg white
Dash of grenadine

Pour the gin, both vermouths, and orange juice into a shaker
two-thirds full of ice cubes; add the egg white. Shake very well.
Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Drop the grenadine on top.
