Whisky Fizz
2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
1 ounce fresh lemon juice
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

5 to 6 ounces club soda
Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the whisky, lemon juice, and simple syrup into a shaker
two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled wine
goblet. Add the club soda; stir briefly. Add the garnish of choice.

Whisky Mac
A Scottish antidote for a cold winter night.
2 ounces scotch
1 ounce green ginger wine

Build in an ice-filled Rocks glass.

Whisky Rickey
2 1/2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
1 ounce fresh lime juice
5 to 6 ounces club soda
Lime wedge, for garnish

W

Pour the whisky and lime juice into an ice-filled Highball glass.
Add the club soda; stir briefly. Add the lime wedge.

Whisky Sangaree
2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
1/2 ounce ruby port
1/2 ounce Yukon Jack liqueur

Freshly grated nutmeg, for garnish

Pour the whisky, port, and Yukon Jack into a mixing glass twothirds full of ice cubes; stir well. Strain into a chilled wine goblet.
Sprinkle with the nutmeg.
