Vodka Collins
2 ounces vodka
1/2 ounce fresh lemon juice
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

5 to 6 ounces club soda
Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the vodka, lemon juice, and simple syrup into a shaker twothirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into an ice-filled Collins
glass. Add the club soda; stir briefly. Add the garnish of choice.
