Simple Syrup
MAKES 4 CUPS (1 QUART)

3 cups water
3 cups granulated sugar
Heat the water in a saucepan set over moderately high heat.
When it begins to simmer, add the sugar and stir until it dissolves. Do not let the mixture boil. Remove the pan from the
heat and set aside to cool to room temperature. Pour the simple syrup through a funnel into an empty, clean, 1-liter liquor
bottle and cap tightly. Store in the refrigerator.
