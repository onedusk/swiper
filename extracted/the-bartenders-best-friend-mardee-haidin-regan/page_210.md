Kamikaze
This drink started out as a shooter but has turned more
respectable; it’s now a sipping cocktail.
2 ounces vodka

Kentucky Champagne Cocktail
1 sugar cube
2 to 3 dashes Peychaud’s bitters
1 ounce bourbon
5 ounces champagne or other sparkling wine
Lemon twist, for garnish

Drop the sugar cube into the bottom of a Champagne Flute; add
the bitters and bourbon. Carefully pour in the champagne. Add
the twist.
