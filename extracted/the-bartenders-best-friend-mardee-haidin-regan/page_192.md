Hot Buttered Rum
Americans have been enjoying Hot Buttered Rum for
over 150 years.
2 ounces dark rum
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

3 whole cloves
1 cinnamon stick (about 3 inches long)
4 to 5 ounces boiling water
2 teaspoons unsalted butter
Freshly grated nutmeg, for garnish

Pour the rum and simple syrup into an Irish Coffee glass. Add the
cloves and cinnamon stick. Add the boiling water to almost fill the
glass. Add the butter; stir briefly. Sprinkle with the nutmeg.
