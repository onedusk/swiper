The Other B & B
1 1/2 ounces Baileys Irish cream liqueur
1 1/2 ounces brandy or cognac

Pour both ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes.
Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

The Social
—Created by King Cocktail Dale DeGroff, New York City, 1998.

2 ounces Jack Daniel’s Tennessee whiskey
3/4 ounce fresh lemon juice

T

1/2 ounce Cherry Heering
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

Maraschino cherry and a lemon twist, for garnishes

Pour the Jack Daniel’s, lemon juice, Cherry Heering, and simple
syrup into a shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well.
Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Add the cherry and twist.

Third Rail Cocktail
There used to be two versions of this drink, but this formula is the one that withstood the test of time.
1 ounce dark rum
1 ounce applejack
1 ounce brandy
2 dashes of absinthe substitute, such as Absente, Pernod,
Herbsaint, or Ricard

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
