Liquid Heroin

Long Beach Iced Tea
1/2 ounce vodka
1/2 ounce gin
1/2 ounce light rum
1/2 ounce tequila
1/2 ounce triple sec
1/2 ounce fresh lemon juice

1 1/2 ounces cranberry juice

Pour the vodka, gin, rum, tequila, triple sec, and both juices into
a shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into an
ice-filled Highball glass. Add the lemon wedge.
