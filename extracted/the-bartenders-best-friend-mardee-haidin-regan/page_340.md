Widow’s Dream Cocktail
More like a widow’s nightmare if you ask me.
1 1/2 ounces Bénédictine
1 egg
Dollop of whipped cream

Pour the Bénédictine into a shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes;
add the egg. Shake very well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
Top with the whipped cream.

Widow’s Kiss #1
The Widow’s Kiss is a Prohibition-era drink created in
Europe while Americans were enduring the Great
Drought.
1 ounce calvados
1/2 ounce yellow Chartreuse
1/2 ounce Bénédictine

Dash of Angostura bitters

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
