Rob Roy
This drink was created at the Waldorf-Astoria Hotel, New
York, and named for the 1913 Broadway musical of the
same name.
2 1/2 ounces scotch
1/2 ounce sweet vermouth

Maraschino cherry, for garnish

Pour the scotch and vermouth into a mixing glass two-thirds full
of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Add the
cherry.

Rob Roy, Dry
2 1/2 ounces scotch
1/2 ounce dry vermouth

Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the scotch and vermouth into a mixing glass two-thirds full
of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Add the
twist.

Rob Roy, Perfect
2 1/2 ounces scotch
1/4 ounce sweet vermouth
1/4 ounce dry vermouth

Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the scotch and both vermouths into a mixing glass twothirds full of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail
glass. Add the twist.

Robotussin
It’s sure to kill a cough—automatically.
1 1/2 ounces cherry brandy
1 1/2 ounces root beer schnapps

Pour both ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Pony glass or Cocktail glass.
