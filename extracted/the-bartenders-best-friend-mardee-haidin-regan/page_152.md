Deauville Cocktail

1 ounce fresh lemon juice

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice

Debonair Cocktail
A wonderful cocktail that’s made with single malt
scotch.

1 ounce Original Canton Delicate Ginger liqueur

D

Pour the scotch and ginger liqueur into a mixing glass two-thirds

Delmonico
Reportedly created at Delmonico’s bar in New York City,
sometime prior to 1917.
1 1/2 ounces gin
1 1/2 ounces dry vermouth
Dash of orange bitters
2 orange twists, for garnish

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full
of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
Add the twists.
