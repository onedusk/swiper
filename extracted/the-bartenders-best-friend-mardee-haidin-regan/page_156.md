Dreamy Dorini Smoking Martini
—Adapted from a recipe by LibationGoddess, Audrey Saunders, 2001.
1/2 ounce Laphroaig 10-year-old scotch

2 ounces Grey Goose vodka
2 to 3 drops of Pernod
Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the scotch, vodka, and Pernod into a shaker two-thirds full
of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Add
the twist.

Dubliner
—From the Ardent Spirits e-letter: Volume 1, Issue 2, March 1999.

2 ounces Bushmill’s Malt Irish whiskey
1/2 ounce sweet vermouth
1/2 ounce Grand Marnier

Green maraschino cherry, for garnish

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass. Add the
cherry.
