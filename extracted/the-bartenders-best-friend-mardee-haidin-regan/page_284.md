Sazerac
The Sazerac was created in New Orleans in the mid1800s and originally contained brandy as a base liquor.
Rye whiskey became the base later in that century, and
bourbon replaced rye, probably right after Prohibition.

S

Dash of absinthe substitute, such as Absente, Pernod,
Herbsaint, or Ricard
2 ounces bourbon
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

2 dashes of Peychaud bitters
Lemon twist, for garnish

Coat the interior of a Rocks glass with the absinthe substitute.
Fill the glass with crushed ice. Pour the bourbon, simple syrup,
and bitters into a mixing glass two-thirds full of ice cubes. Stir
well. Strain into the glass. Add the twist.
