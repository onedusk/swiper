M

—Adapted from a recipe by Manne Hinojosa, Walnut Creek Yacht Club,
Walnut Creek, California.

1 1/2 ounces Pearl Vodka
1/2 ounce Cointreau
1/2 ounce peach schnapps
1/2 ounce fresh lime juice
1/4 ounce blue curaçao

Lime twist, for garnish

Pour the vodka, Cointreau, schnapps, and lime juice into a
shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into a
chilled Cocktail glass. Gently pour the blue curaçao down the
side of the glass so that it rests on the bottom. Add the twist.
