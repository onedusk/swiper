Leap Year
—Created on February 29, 1928, at London’s Savoy Hotel.

2 ounces gin
1/2 ounce sweet vermouth
1/2 ounce Grand Marnier
1/4 ounce fresh lemon juice

Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the gin, vermouth, Grand Marnier, and lemon juice into a
shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into a
chilled Cocktail glass. Add the twist.

Lemon Drop

will tell you, this is a great drink served straight up or on

into a shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into
