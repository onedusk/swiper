Pink Lady
The original 1930 recipe for this drink didn’t call for
heavy cream, so this version is a revised formula.
2 ounces gin
1/2 ounce heavy cream

2 dashes of grenadine
1 egg white

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Shake very well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
