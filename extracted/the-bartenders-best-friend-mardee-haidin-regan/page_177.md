Gin Crusta
Superfine sugar and a lemon wedge,
for rimming the glass
Lemon peel spiral (see technique, page 38)
2 ounces gin
1/2 ounce curaçao
1/4 ounce fresh lemon juice

Rim a Sour glass using the lemon wedge and sugar. Place the
lemon peel spiral into the glass so that it lines almost the entire
interior.
Pour the gin, curaçao, and lemon juice into a shaker two-thirds
full of crushed ice. Shake well; strain into the glass.

Gin Daisy
This Daisy is considered to be the classic.
2 1/2 ounces gin
1 ounce fresh lemon juice
1/2 ounce grenadine

Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the gin, lemon juice, and grenadine into a shaker two-thirds
full of crushed ice. Shake well. Strain into a crushed ice–filled
Highball glass. Add the twist.

Gin Fix
This Fix is considered to be the classic.
2 1/2 ounces gin
1 ounce fresh lemon juice
1/2 ounce pineapple juice

Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the gin, lemon juice, and pineapple juice into a shaker
two-thirds full of crushed ice. Shake well. Strain into a crushed
ice–filled Highball glass. Add the garnish of choice.
