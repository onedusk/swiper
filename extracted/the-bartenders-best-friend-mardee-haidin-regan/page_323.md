Vanilla Coke
1 1/2 ounces Stolichnaya Vanil vodka
4 ounces Coca-Cola

Build in an ice-filled Highball glass. Stir with a sip-stick.

Vanilla Cream
1 1/2 ounces Baileys Irish cream liqueur
1 1/2 ounces Stolichnaya Vanil vodka

Pour both ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes.
Shake well. Strain into an ice-filled Rocks glass or a chilled
Cocktail glass.
