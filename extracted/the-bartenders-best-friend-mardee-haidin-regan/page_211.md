Kentucky Distillery Punch
This punch packs a wallop—feel free to dilute it with
more club soda or some ginger ale.
MAKES ABOUT 2 4 SIX-OUNCE SERVINGS

1 750-ml bottle bourbon
1 750-ml bottle dark rum
1 750-ml bottle brandy
6 ounces simple syrup (page 33)
1 cup fresh lemon juice
2 ounces grenadine
1 large block of ice
16 ounces ginger ale

Pour the bourbon, rum, brandy, simple syrup, lemon juice, and
grenadine into a nonreactive large pan or bowl; stir well. Cover
and refrigerate until chilled, at least 4 hours.
Place the ice in the center of a large punch bowl; add the punch.
Pour in the ginger ale.
