Tequila Conquistador

Tequila Cooler
2 1/2 ounces tequila
6 to 7 ounces lemon-lime soda

T

Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the tequila and the lemon-lime soda into an ice-filled
Collins glass. Stir briefly. Add the twist.

Tequila Fix
2 1/2 ounces tequila
1 ounce fresh lime juice
1/2 ounce pineapple juice

Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the tequila, lime juice, and pineapple juice into a shaker
two-thirds full of crushed ice. Shake well. Strain into a crushed
ice–filled Highball glass. Add the garnish of choice.

Tequila Fizz
2 ounces tequila
1 ounce fresh lime juice
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

5 to 6 ounces club soda
Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the tequila, lime juice, and simple syrup into a shaker twothirds full of ice cubes. Shake well; strain into a chilled wine goblet. Add the club soda; stir briefly. Add the garnish of choice.
