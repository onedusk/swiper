Whisky Buck
1 lemon wedge
2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
5 ounces ginger ale

Squeeze the lemon wedge into a Highball glass and drop it into
the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes. Add the whisky and ginger ale. Stir briefly.

Whisky Cobbler
2 1/2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

Pour the whisky and simple syrup into a wine goblet filled with
crushed ice. Stir briefly. Add the garnish of choice.

Whisky Collins
2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
1/2 ounce fresh lemon juice
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

5 to 6 ounces club soda
Fresh fruit in season, for garnish

W

Pour the whisky, lemon juice, and simple syrup into a shaker
two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into an ice-filled
Collins glass. Add the club soda; stir briefly. Add the garnish of
choice.

Whisky Cooler
2 1/2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
6 to 7 ounces ginger ale
Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the whisky and ginger ale into an ice-filled Collins glass.
Stir briefly; add the twist.
