Brunch Punch
Preparing brunch tends to be a busy time for the host,
so serving a premade punch is an excellent idea.
MAKES ABOUT 24 SIX-OUNCE SERVINGS

8 ounces vodka

B

8 ounces peach schnapps
4 ounces Chambord raspberry liqueur
1/2 gallon orange juice

2 liters ginger ale or lemon-lime soda
1 block of ice

Chill all of the ingredients ahead of time. Mix together in a large
punch bowl and add the ice.

Bubble Gum Shooter
1/2 ounce vodka
1/2 ounce crème de banane
1/2 ounce peach schnapps
1/2 ounce orange juice

Layer the ingredients in a Pony glass.

Buck’s Fizz
Named for the Buck’s Club, London.
2 ounces orange juice
4 ounces champagne or other sparkling wine
Dash of grenadine
Orange slice, for garnish

Pour the orange juice into a Champagne Flute; carefully add the
champagne or sparkling wine. Drizzle the grenadine on top. Add
the orange slice.
