Royal Champagne Cocktail

5 ounces champagne or other sparkling wine

Royal Mimosa
1/4 ounce brandy
1/4 ounce Grand Marnier

1 ounce orange juice
4 ounces champagne or other sparkling wine
Orange wheel, for garnish

Pour the brandy, Grand Marnier, orange juice, and champagne
into a Champagne Flute. Stir briefly. Add the orange wheel.

Ruby Martini
A gem of a cocktail.
2 1/2 ounces gin
1/4 ounce dry vermouth
1/4 ounce cherry brandy

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
