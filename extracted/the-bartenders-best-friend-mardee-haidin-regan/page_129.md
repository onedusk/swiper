Caruso
Named for the late opera tenor Enrico Caruso, this drink
used to be made with equal amounts of all three ingredients. It works far better this way; it’s a drink that will
make you sing.
2 ounces gin
1/2 ounce dry vermouth

Dash of green crème de menthe

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
