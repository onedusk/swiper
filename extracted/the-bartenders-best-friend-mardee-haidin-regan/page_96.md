Black Widow

1 1/2 ounces black sambuca

Pour both ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Blackthorne
2 ounces Irish whiskey
3/4 ounce sweet vermouth

2 dashes of Pernod
2 dashes of Angostura bitters
Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the whiskey, vermouth, Pernod, and bitters into a mixing
glass two-thirds full of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled
Cocktail glass. Add the twist.
