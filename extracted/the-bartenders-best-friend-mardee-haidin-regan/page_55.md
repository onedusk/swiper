Specific Densities
When layering ingredients for a Pousse-Café, pour the spirit with the
highest specific gravity as the bottom layer and top it with successively
less dense spirits. Thus, according to this chart, which lists the specific
densities of liqueurs in the Marie Brizard line plus a few other brands,
you might create a layered drink that is clear, topped with red, topped
with blue, and finished with a final layer of clear—white crème de
cacao, strawberry liqueur, blue curaçao, and either triple sec or vodka.
Remember, heaviest liqueurs on the bottom, lighter ones on top.
Note: These spirits are listed in descending order, from the heaviest to
the lightest.
