Preakness Cocktail
In 1873, the year after a horse named Preakness won the
very first race to be held at the Pimlico racetrack,
Governor Oden Bowie of Maryland, a horseman and racing entrepreneur, named the then new race for threeyear-olds after the horse. The word Preakness comes
from the language of the Minisi, a northern New Jersey
tribe of Native Americans, who called their area Pra-quales, meaning quail woods. The name just evolved into
Preakness.
2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
1 ounce sweet vermouth
2 dashes of Bénédictine
Dash of Angostura bitters
Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the whisky, vermouth, Bénédictine, and bitters into a mixing
glass two-thirds full of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled
Cocktail glass. Add the twist.
