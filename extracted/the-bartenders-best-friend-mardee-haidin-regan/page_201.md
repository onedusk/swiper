Irish Peach Martini

I

Irish Raspberry Martini

Irish Rickey
2 1/2 ounces Irish whiskey
1 ounce fresh lime juice
5 to 6 ounces club soda
Lime wedge, for garnish

Pour the whiskey and lime juice into an ice-filled Highball glass.
Add the club soda; stir briefly. Add the lime wedge.

Irish Sangaree
With just a touch of honey from the Irish Mist liqueur,
and the aroma from the nutmeg garnish, this is a complex potion.
2 ounces Irish whiskey
1/2 ounce ruby port
1/2 ounce Irish Mist liqueur

Freshly grated nutmeg, for garnish

Pour the whiskey, port, and Irish Mist into a mixing glass
two-thirds full of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled wine
goblet. Sprinkle with the nutmeg.
