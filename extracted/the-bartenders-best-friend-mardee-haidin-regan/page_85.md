Banff Cocktail
When in Canada. . . . For a drier version of this drink, try
using Cointreau instead of curaçao.
2 ounces blended Canadian whisky
1/2 ounce curaçao

B

1/2 ounce cherry brandy

Dash of Angostura bitters

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Banshee
1 ounce crème de banane
1 ounce white crème de cacao
1 ounce heavy cream

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
