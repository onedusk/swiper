CEO Cocktail
Just for big shots.
—Adapted from a recipe by Dr. Cocktail (Ted Haigh).

2 ounces brandy
1 ounce Lillet Blanc
1/2 ounce Chambord or crème de cassis

2 dashes of orange bitters
Lemon twist, for garnish

Pour the brandy, Lillet, Chambord, and orange bitters into a
shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain into a
chilled Cocktail glass. Add the lemon twist.
