Spiced Cranberry-Citrus Punch
MAKES ABOUT 18 SIX-OUNCE SERVINGS

1 quart cranberry juice

S

16 ounces orange juice
16 ounces tangerine juice
5 whole cloves
2 cinnamon sticks (each about 3 inches long), broken
1 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
1 large block of ice, for serving
Mandarin orange segments, for garnish

Pour the juices into a large saucepan set over high heat. Add the
cloves, cinnamon sticks, and nutmeg and bring the mixture to a
boil. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes.
Strain the mixture through a sieve lined with a double layer of
dampened cheesecloth; discard the solids. Set aside to cool to
room temperature. Cover and refrigerate until chilled.
Place the block of ice into a punch bowl. Add the chilled punch.
Add the garnishes.
