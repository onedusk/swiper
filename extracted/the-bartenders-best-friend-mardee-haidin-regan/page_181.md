Glad Tidings Glögg
Flaming this Swedish drink provides quite a show, but
be very careful while lighting it.
MAKES 6 SIX-OUNCE SERVINGS

1 750-ml bottle brandy
4 ounces simple syrup (page 33)
8 whole cloves
1/2 cup raisins
1/2 cup blanched almonds

8 ounces ruby port

Place the brandy, simple syrup, cloves, raisins, and almonds into
a large saucepan set over moderately low heat. Cook until warm.
Ignite the liquid with a match, and allow it to flame for about 15
seconds. Add the port and stir constantly with a long-handled
wooden spoon until the flames subside. Ladle into Irish Coffee
glasses, making sure that each glass contains some of the
raisins and almonds.

glass. Add the cherry.
