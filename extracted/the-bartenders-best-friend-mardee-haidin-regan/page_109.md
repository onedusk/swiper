Bourbon Sour
2 ounces bourbon
3/4 ounce fresh lemon juice
1/2 ounce simple syrup (page 33)

Orange wheel and maraschino cherry, for garnish

Pour the bourbon, lemon juice, and simple syrup into a shaker
two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain the drink into a
chilled Sour glass. Add the orange and cherry garnishes.

B

Bourbon Stinger
2 ounces bourbon
1/2 ounce white crème de menthe

Pour both ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice cubes.
Shake well. Strain into a crushed ice–filled wine goblet.

Bourbon Swizzle
2 ounces bourbon
1/2 ounce fresh lemon juice
1/2 ounce apricot brandy

5 to 6 ounces ginger ale
Lemon wheel, for garnish

Pour the bourbon, lemon juice, and apricot brandy into a shaker
two-thirds full of ice cubes. Shake well. Strain the mixture into an
ice-filled Collins glass. Add the ginger ale; stir briefly. Add the
lemon wheel and a swizzle stick.

Brain Damage
1 ounce Jägermeister
3/4 ounce gin
1/2 ounce vodka

Pour all of the ingredients into a mixing glass two-thirds full of
ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Pony glass.
