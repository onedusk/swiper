Rosebud
“‘Rosebud.’ The most famous word in the history of cinema. It explains everything, and nothing. Who, for that
matter, actually heard Charles Foster Kane say it before
he died?” —Roger Ebert
2 ounces red or pink grapefruit juice
2 ounces citrus vodka
1/2 ounce triple sec

1 ounce lime juice cordial, such as Rose’s

Pour the grapefruit juice into an ice-filled Collins glass. Pour the
vodka, triple sec, and lime juice cordial into a shaker two-thirds
full of ice. Shake well. Strain into the Collins glass so the mixture
floats on the grapefruit juice.
