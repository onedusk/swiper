Gin & It
The “It” in the Gin & It refers to the sweet vermouth that
often is referred to as “Italian,” since Italy was the birthplace of sweet vermouth.
3 ounces gin
1/2 ounce sweet vermouth

Pour the gin and the vermouth into a mixing glass two-thirds full
of ice cubes. Stir well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.

Gin & Sin
2 ounces gin
1 ounce orange juice
1/2 ounce fresh lemon juice

2 dashes of grenadine

Pour all of the ingredients into a shaker two-thirds full of ice
cubes. Shake well. Strain into a chilled Cocktail glass.
