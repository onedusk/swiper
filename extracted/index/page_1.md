Stripe Atlas C Corporation Guide

This guide provides an overview of forming a C Corporation with Stripe Atlas, including cost and structuring
a holding company.

Cost

Stripe Atlas offers a straightforward pricing model for incorporation.
Initial Cost: A one-time fee of $500. This includes:
Company incorporation in Delaware.
First year of registered agent service.
Tax ID (EIN) registration.
Founder equity issuance and 83(b) election filing.
Annual Cost: $100 per year for the registered agent service, which is required to maintain good
standing in Delaware.
Start a US company from anywhere in the world using Stripe Atlas.
Use Stripe Atlas to incorporate your company in Delaware, obtain your company tax ID (EIN) from the IRS,
issue founders equity, and file your 83(b) election. After you incorporate your business, you can open a
business bank account and charge customers through Stripe payments. Learn more about Atlas.
Stripe Atlas doesn’t provide legal, tax, or accounting advice. If you have any unique considerations,
consider talking to legal counsel before proceeding.

Form your company

How to incorporate your company: Learn what you need to get started with Atlas.
Company types: Use Atlas to form either a Delaware Limited Liability Company (LLC) or a Delaware C
corporation.
Incorporation documents: Learn about the documents Atlas uses to incorporate your company.
Section 83(b) elections: Learn about common Section 83(b) considerations.
Non-US Section 83(b) elections: Learn about filing a Section 83(b) election as a non-US founder.
Founder equity terms: Learn how to select your equity vesting terms.

Post-formation resources

Business bank accounts: Open a business bank account with a Stripe Atlas partner.
Business taxes: Learn about business taxes and how to prepare your LLC or C corporation for tax season.
Accept payments after incorporating: Use Stripe’s no code payment options and get one year of free
payments processing.
