Holding Company and Subsidiaries

Stripe Atlas supports the creation of complex business structures, such as a holding company with
subsidiaries. This is a common strategy for businesses looking to isolate liability, manage different business
lines, or optimize for investment.

Strategy

1. Form the Holding Company: First, incorporate a C Corporation that will serve as the parent
(holding) company. This can be done directly through the standard Stripe Atlas application.
2. Form Subsidiaries: Once the holding company is established, you can form additional C
Corporations as subsidiaries. When filling out the Atlas application for a subsidiary, you will
designate the holding company as the parent company.
3. Issue Shares: After the subsidiary is incorporated, you will need to issue its shares to the parent
company. Stripe Atlas provides a standard template for this, which you can customize.
