ESP-Matter Programming Guide, Release latest

Matter is a unified IP-based connectivity protocol that is designed to connect and build open, reliable and secure IoT
ecosystems. This new technology and connectivity standard enables communication among a wide range of smart devices.
Matter supports IP connectivity over Wi-Fi, Thread and Ethernet.
Espressif’s SDK for Matter is the official Matter development framework for Espressif’s ESP32 series SoCs.
We have put together a series of blog posts that introduces various aspects of Matter. We recommend that you go through
this Espressif Matter Blog.
